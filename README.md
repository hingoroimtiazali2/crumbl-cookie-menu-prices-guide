# Crumbl Cookie Menu Prices, Flavors and Nutrition — An Independent Guide

This is an independent guide. It is not the official Crumbl website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Crumbl. It was written to help readers understand how the menu is organized, how flavors change, and how to compare choices before ordering.

## How the Crumbl menu works

Crumbl is known for large, bakery-style cookies served in a rotating weekly lineup. A small number of flavors usually stays available for longer, while other flavors appear for a limited time and then return later or are replaced. Because the lineup changes, the best way to plan a visit is to check the current menu for your location, note the flavors you want to try, and decide whether you want a single cookie, a small box, or a larger box to share.

## Understanding menu prices

Prices can vary by location, box size, and the mix of flavors in an order. A single cookie is the simplest way to sample a new flavor, while a box can be a better choice when several people want different flavors or when you want to compare a classic with a seasonal option. Before you order, compare the choices at your location, look for the total for the box size you want, and remember that taxes and delivery charges, where they apply, may change the final amount.

## Flavors to look for

The menu usually includes chocolate-based cookies, sugar cookies, and cookies topped with frosting, fruit, candy, or crumbs. Some flavors are rich and sweet, others are lighter in taste, and some combine warm dough with a cool topping. If you enjoy a familiar flavor, start with a classic. If you want something new, choose one limited-time flavor and one classic so you can compare them side by side. Reading the flavor description before you order helps you spot ingredients you like, toppings you may want to avoid, and textures such as soft, chewy, crisp, or chilled.

## Nutrition and allergens

Cookies are a treat, so portion size matters. Sharing a cookie, choosing a smaller box, or saving part of a cookie for later can make it easier to enjoy the flavor without eating more than you planned. Nutrition information, where published for a flavor, can help you compare options, but it may change when a recipe or topping changes. Always check the current information for the exact flavor and location, and ask staff about common allergens such as wheat, milk, eggs, nuts, and soy before you order if you have a food allergy or sensitivity.
